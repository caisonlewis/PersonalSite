How to Use My WebRcade Feed

WebRcade is a web-based retro gaming platform that allows you to play classic games directly in your browser. This page provides instructions on how to use my custom game feed.

Getting Started

  1. Visit WebRcade in your web browser.
  2. Click on "Feeds" in the WebRcade interface.
  3. Add a new feed by entering the URL to my feed file: webrcadefeed.json.
  4. Once added, you can browse and play the games from my collection.

Note: All the roms are linked via a dropbrox account that may or may not be still servicing some of these roms. They work as of May 2026

Just click "collections" to view the available games and feeds. This should let you add a new feed. Just right click the hyperlink for my json file above to copy the path and paste in as a url for a new feed. Preloaded with tons of roms for tons of systems.
